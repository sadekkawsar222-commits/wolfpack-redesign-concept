#IssueWhereSeverityRecommendation
1The design looks like a stock agency template: centred headings, white sections alternating with grey, and the same three-card grid used for services, cases and blog. Almost nothing on the page moves or responds to the visitor, and "Become Alpha" is never shown visually.SitewideHighGive the site a signature visual system and interactions that carry the "pack" idea. This is what the redesign does.
2The floating "Keep scrolling" mascot sits in the bottom-left corner and covers CTAs and card text as you scroll (seen over "Learn Our Story" and the case-study cards).HomeHighRemove it, or make it dismissible and hide it whenever a CTA scrolls underneath.
329 links have no accessible name: social icons, image-only card links and badge links.Home, footerHighAdd aria-label to icon links. Mark duplicate image links on cards with aria-hidden and tabindex="-1".
4Certification and award badges have empty alt, so screen readers skip trust signals that matter (Google Partner, HubSpot, Clutch).Home, AboutMediumGive real badges descriptive alt text. Put aria-hidden on the marquee's cloned copies.
5A Google review card ("A1 Property Inspection") shows a name and date with no review text.Home reviewsMediumFilter out rating-only reviews, or show the stars on their own.
6Case-study images were still empty dark boxes when the section scrolled into view, so the lazy loading kicks in too late.Home, ResultsMediumStart lazy loading earlier (rootMargin around 600px) and add a low-quality placeholder or a dominant-colour background.
7Images are served much larger than they're displayed: the logo is 594px wide but shown at 160px, the Google Partner badge 299px at 80px, the team photo 1024px at 455px, review avatars 96px at 36px.HomeMediumUse srcset/sizes, WebP or AVIF, and an SVG logo.
8Heavy page: 37 script tags, 2,053 DOM nodes, DOMContentLoaded at 2.5s and load at 4.6s.HomeMediumDefer third-party widgets (reviews, chat) until interaction and combine scripts.
9The long-form SEO guide (five tabbed chapters) and the FAQ sit in the middle of the conversion flow, which pushes reviews and the final CTA down to around 7,500px.HomeMediumKeep the guide but collapse it, and move proof (reviews) higher.
10On mobile, 39 tap targets are under 32px tall, 16 text elements are under 12px, and the two-line announcement bar pushes the hero photo below the fold.MobileMediumMake tap targets at least 44px, set a 14px text floor, and use a one-line announcement bar.
11The site loads four font families (Montserrat, Space Grotesk, Inter, JetBrains Mono).SitewideLowPreload only the display and body weights you use, and subset the rest.
12Results claims "Proof, not promises," but several of the 28 case cards show soft highlights ("Noticeable boost in audience engagement") instead of numbers.ResultsLowPut quantified cases first and label the qualitative ones as qualitative.
13Only one CTA wording ("Get Started"), with no secondary route for visitors still researching, such as viewing results or grading their site.Home heroLowAdd a secondary hero CTA ("See the results") and bring the AEO Grader into the page flow.
14The industries section is a wall of 16 identical pill links with no hierarchy.HomeLowGroup them by vertical (Home Services, Construction, Professional), the way the Industries page already does.
