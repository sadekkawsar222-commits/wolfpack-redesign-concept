Our story

Our story began with frustration.

The founders

Two friends who grew up in home inspection.

Aaron Shishilla was working as a home inspector for his parents' company in Melbourne, Florida. Austin Hintze was running marketing for his dad's inspection company, Waypoint Property Inspection, in Tampa. They met through the industry and became fast friends, bonding over a shared frustration with how digital marketing agencies operated and a shared belief that they could build something better.

The systems

Built from the work, not the pitch deck.

Austin had engineered a marketing automation system at Waypoint that connected scheduling software to a CRM to nurture leads and clients automatically. Aaron was designing websites and learning to drive real results through SEO and paid search.

2019

Started with a simple, stubborn belief.

Local businesses deserve a marketing partner that actually cares. That owns up to the results. And delivers.

Aaron and Austin worked nights. Weekends. They pushed each other and never backed down from a hard problem. They had spent years watching and being burned by agencies doing the same things: overcharging, under-communicating, handing over surface-level work and calling it a strategy, locking clients into contracts, then hiding behind vague reports.

Today

Hundreds of clients across the country.

What started as two friends solving one industry's problem is now a digital marketing agency serving hundreds of clients across the country. The mission never changed: bring clarity, honesty, and real performance back to digital marketing.

What makes us different

Four promises we keep.

Transparency

You own everything

Most agencies block access, lock down permissions, and hand you a custom report they built to look good. We do the opposite. It's your business, and your marketing is your asset. You get full access, full permissions, and clear reporting. Always.

ROI

Actual results

We don't hide behind impressions, clicks, and vanity metrics. We report the numbers that actually move your business: cost per quotable lead, return on ad spend, and full return on investment.

Flexibility
